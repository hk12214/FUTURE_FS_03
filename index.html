About Us

Campus Cravings is a local food spot located at ASTU Stadium, bringing together a variety of food vendors in one convenient place. We serve students, staff, and visitors with fresh, affordable, and delicious meals every day.

Whether you're looking for fast food, traditional dishes, or refreshing drinks, our goal is to make sure you enjoy quality food without long waiting times.

We focus on good service, clean environment, and a comfortable place to relax and enjoy your meal with friends.
